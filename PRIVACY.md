# Privacy Policy — Project Tracker

**Last updated: 18 September 2026**

Project Tracker ("the app") is designed so that your data stays yours. This
policy explains what the app stores, where it lives, and what leaves your
devices.

## Summary

- The developer does **not** collect, receive, see, or sell any of your data.
- There are **no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party
  tracking** in the app.
- Everything you create stays in **your own iCloud and on your own devices**.

## What the app stores

The app stores the information you enter so it can show your project stages
and sync them between your devices:

- Your projects: their names, stages, tasks, dates, and which tasks you have
  checked off.
- Notes you write for each stage.
- PDF files you attach to a stage or to your reference library.
- Your settings, such as your reminder preferences and target buffer.

## Where your data is stored

- **On your device**, in the app's storage.
- **In your personal iCloud account** (iCloud Drive and iCloud Key-Value
  storage), so it syncs across your own Mac and iPhone.

Your iCloud data is governed by Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy. The
developer has no access to your iCloud account or its contents.

## What the app does *not* do

- It does not send your data to the developer or to any server run by the
  developer.
- It does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- It does not require an account, email address, or sign-in.
- Notifications ("daily reminders") are scheduled locally on your device.

## Data you choose to share

The app lets you **export a project as a JSON file** so you can hand it to an
AI assistant (such as ChatGPT or Claude) to help tailor the template to your
project. When you do this, you are choosing to share that file with a service
you selected. That service is operated by a third party and is covered by
**that service's own privacy policy**, not this one. The app never uploads
anything on your behalf — export and sharing are always actions you take
yourself.

## Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a
new "Last updated" date.
