## Category

Productivity (primary). Education (secondary) is a reasonable fit given the academic audience.

## Description

Project Tracker keeps a thesis, dissertation, final-year project, or any smaller personal project moving through its stages.

Lay out the stages you actually have to get through, each with its real deadline, its weighting, and a short checklist. The app tells you what is next, what is running, and what is overdue, and shows a personal target date a few days ahead of the official one so you finish with room to spare.

WHAT YOU GET
• Stages with deadlines, weightings and task checklists
• A "next up" card and an urgent banner for anything due within three days
• Your own target buffer: aim 1 to 7 days ahead of each deadline
• Notes plus draft and final PDFs attached to every stage
• A reference library for papers, links and notes
• Daily reminders per stage, on the days and time you choose
• Home-screen widget in three sizes, pinnable to any project
• Several projects side by side
• iCloud sync between your iPhone, iPad and Mac

MAKE IT YOURS IN MINUTES
New projects start from a generic template. Export it as a file, give it to an AI assistant together with your module handbook or milestone list, and import the result. Your ticked-off progress is kept.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no analytics, no server. Your projects live in your own iCloud Drive and on your devices.

## Review notes (suggested)

The app works fully offline and without an iCloud account. To see it with content, tap New Project and give it any name. The AI-tailoring step is optional and happens outside the app: the user exports a JSON file, edits it with any assistant, and imports it back. No login is required anywhere.
